Projects

SnowPass

checkthepass.com

Web app surfacing live weather, traffic-camera footage, and DOT updates along a driving route.

Role: Software Developer & Lead Contact

Team: 5-person senior Computer Science project team

Description

Winter travel through northern Nevada means heavy snowfall, high winds, and icy roads, and the information drivers need is scattered across multiple sources. SnowPass consolidates it into one tool: select any two points in California or Nevada, and it surfaces the best route along with route-specific weather, live traffic-camera footage, and Department of Transportation updates, so drivers can make informed decisions before and during a trip.

Steel Bridge Inspection Robot

Robot built to inspect steel bridge structures for defects and material degradation

Role: Business Analyst & Software Specialist

Team: 3-person team with a civil engineer and an electrical engineer

Description

Developed at the Advanced Robotics and Automation (ARA) Lab at the University of Nevada, Reno, this robot climbs steel bridge structures to collect visual and eddy-current data. Eddy-current sensing detects defects and deterioration below the surface, and the data is compiled into visual maps showing the structure's condition.
